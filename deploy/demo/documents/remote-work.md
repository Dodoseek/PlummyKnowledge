# Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days a week after agreeing the schedule with their manager.

Plum Orchard Ltd. is a fictional company; this handbook exists only for the PlummyKnowledge demo.

## Equipment

- The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset for the home office.
- A one-time home office allowance of 300 EUR covers a chair or a desk.

## Security at home

- Lock the screen when you leave the laptop, even at home.
- Use the company VPN for internal systems; never forward work email to a personal account.
- Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within one hour.
