# Onboarding

New employees get their laptop, accounts and a buddy on the first day, and complete onboarding within four weeks.

Plum Orchard Ltd. is a fictional company; this handbook exists only for the PlummyKnowledge demo.

## First week

- Meet your buddy and your manager; agree goals for the first 90 days.
- Complete the security awareness course before accessing customer data.
- Read the travel and remote work policies in this handbook.

## Vacation

- Full-time employees have 28 days of paid vacation per year.
- Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.
