# Travel policy

Hotels are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night in capital cities and up to 130 EUR per night elsewhere.

Plum Orchard Ltd. is a fictional company; this handbook exists only for the PlummyKnowledge demo.

## Booking

- Book flights and hotels through the travel desk at least 14 days before the trip.
- Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours; business class needs approval from a director.
- Trains are preferred over flights for trips under four hours.

## Expenses

- Meals are covered up to 50 EUR per day with receipts.
- Taxis are reimbursed between the airport or station and the hotel, and late at night.
- Submit the expense report within 30 days after the trip, with all receipts attached.
