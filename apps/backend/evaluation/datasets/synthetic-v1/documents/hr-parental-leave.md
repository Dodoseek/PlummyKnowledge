# Parental Leave Policy

## Paid leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives
4 weeks of fully paid leave.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible after six months of employment. Leave must start within 12 months of the birth
or adoption.

## Returning to work

During the first month after returning, employees may work part-time at full pay.
